## What Are 12306 Points and How to Earn Them?

**12306 points** are membership reward points offered by China Railway Customer Service Center for frequent travelers. According to railway regulations:

- **Point accumulation**: Earn 5 points per 1 RMB spent (ticket face value × 5)
- **Validity**: Points remain valid for 12 consecutive months from the date they enter your account
- **Redemption threshold**: First-time redemption requires a minimum of 10,000 points
- **Redemption rate**: 100 points = 1 RMB

Point sources include: purchasing train tickets, participating in official railway promotions, and linking partner platforms (such as Fliggy).

## What Are the Rules for Free Business Class Meals?

Not all business class seats include meals. The following **three conditions** must be met simultaneously:

| Condition | Specific Requirements | Notes |
|-----------|----------------------|-------|
| **Meal time windows** | Breakfast before 7:30, Lunch 11:30-13:00, Dinner 17:30-19:00 | Only when train operation covers meal times |
| **Cross-bureau trains** | Origin and destination stations under different railway bureaus | e.g., Guangzhou Bureau → Shanghai Bureau, not same-bureau routes |
| **Business class** | Hold a business class ticket | First and second class seats do not include meals |

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1: Is redeeming business class with 12306 points more cost-effective than cash purchase?**
A: It depends on point acquisition cost. If points were accumulated from business trips (company reimbursed), it's essentially a free upgrade; if from self-funded purchases, the 100 points = 1 RMB rate is equivalent to cash, but points have a 12-month validity—recommend using before expiration.

**Q2: Can business class meals be taken to-go?**
A: Yes. If you cannot dine on board due to your itinerary, you may request attendants to package the meal for takeaway. Meal boxes use disposable eco-friendly packaging.

**Q3: Can short trips (e.g., 15 minutes) still receive meals?**
A: Yes. As long as cross-bureau + meal time window + business class conditions are met, meals are provided regardless of travel duration. This Hangzhou East-Linping South trip was only 15 minutes yet included a full lunch.

**Q4: How to check if a train is cross-bureau?**
A: Check the origin/destination stations on your ticket or the 12306 app. Major railway bureau jurisdictions:
- **Guangzhou Bureau**: Guangdong, Hunan, Hainan
- **Shanghai Bureau**: Shanghai, Jiangsu, Zhejiang, Anhui
- **Beijing Bureau**: Beijing, Tianjin, parts of Hebei
- **Other bureaus**: Chengdu, Wuhan, Xi'an, etc.

**Q5: Can business class tickets redeemed with points accumulate more points?**
A: No. Tickets obtained through point redemption do not earn additional travel points; only cash-paid portions accumulate points.
