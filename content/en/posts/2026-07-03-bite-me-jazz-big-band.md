## The full sound of a big band

This video records Bite Me Jazz Big Band at FF Jazz Festival in Zhuantang BAC, Hangzhou, on May 24, 2026.

The appeal of a big band is the stacking of sections: arrangement, unison hits, and forward motion. Compared with smaller jazz groups, the energy is more direct; you are not just following one melody, but standing inside a wider sound field.

This is one of the more open and energetic moments in the FF Jazz series.
