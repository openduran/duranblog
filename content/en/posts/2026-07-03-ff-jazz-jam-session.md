## The improvised finale of the festival

This video records the Jam Session at FF Jazz Festival in Zhuantang BAC, Hangzhou, on June 7, 2026.

A jam session is an important form in jazz culture. It is not only about a fixed song or a complete arrangement; it is about interaction, handoff, risk, and response in the moment. A phrase appears, another player takes it forward, and the performance opens from there.

As a closing clip for the series, it leaves the festival with a sense of openness, immediacy, and shared participation.
