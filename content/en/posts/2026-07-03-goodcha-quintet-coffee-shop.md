## Jazz in a coffee-shop mood

This video features Goodcha Quintet performing Coffee Shop at FF Jazz Festival in Zhuantang BAC, Hangzhou.

The title already sets the tone: coffee shop, neighborhood, weekend festival, and a small jazz ensemble sharing the same space. The quintet format gives the music enough layers while keeping the performance relaxed and close to the audience.

It feels like a street-corner cup of coffee: simple, warm, and worth sitting with for a few minutes.
