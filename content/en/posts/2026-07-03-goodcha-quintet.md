## The layered sound of a quintet

This is another live record of Goodcha Quintet at FF Jazz Festival in Zhuantang BAC, Hangzhou.

A quintet is a strong format for jazz: thicker than a duo or trio, more flexible than a big band, and spacious enough for each voice to breathe. In a community setting, that balance gives the music structure while keeping its live looseness.

The clip works as a steady anchor in this FF Jazz series: one neighborhood, one stage, and a band with a clear ensemble feel.
