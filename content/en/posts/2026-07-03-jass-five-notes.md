## A live cut built from five notes

This video records Jass Five Notes at FF Jazz Festival in Zhuantang BAC, Hangzhou, on May 30, 2026.

The name Five Notes suggests short motifs, repeated phrases, and improvised development. Jazz does not always need to start from something large; it can unfold from a small musical idea in front of the audience.
