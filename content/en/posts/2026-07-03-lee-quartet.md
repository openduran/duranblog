## The balance of a quartet

This video features Lee Quartet at FF Jazz Festival in Zhuantang BAC, Hangzhou, on May 24, 2026.

The quartet format is classic and flexible. It keeps rhythm, harmony, and melody in balance without crowding the sound. In this live setting, the music has enough drive and enough room to breathe.

The focus is not a single flashy moment, but the balance between players and the way the ensemble holds together.
