## The space inside a duo

This video records Moment Duo at FF Jazz Festival in Zhuantang BAC, Hangzhou, on May 23, 2026.

A duo leaves room. With fewer players, every entrance, pause, and response becomes clearer. The performance feels like a direct conversation, where the audience can hear both the musical structure and the decisions being made in the moment.

Many live passages happen only once. This clip keeps that passing moment available to revisit.
