## A cool current through the live set

This video captures Siria Cold Air performing at FF Jazz Festival in Zhuantang BAC, Hangzhou, on May 17, 2026.

The appeal of a jazz set is not only the melody. It is also the way musicians observe each other, catch each phrase, and push the improvisation forward. In this clip, the performance keeps that sense of movement from beginning to end.

The neighborhood setting makes the music feel open: the audience can move close, and the sound becomes part of the surrounding air.
