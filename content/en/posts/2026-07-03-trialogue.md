## Music as a three-way dialogue

This video features Trialogue at FF Jazz Festival in Zhuantang BAC, Hangzhou, on May 31, 2026.

The name already points to conversation. One of the most engaging parts of a jazz performance is that players are not only playing together; they are answering one another, turning a phrase into a new direction.

The clip keeps that layered sense of sound, image, and live response.
