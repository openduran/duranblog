### 2. Google AdSense

This website uses **Google AdSense** to display advertisements. As a third-party vendor, Google uses cookies to serve ads on this site and other sites:

- Google uses DART cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites
- Users may opt out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting the [Google ad settings](https://adssettings.google.com)
- For more information, please read the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

This website uses cookies to:

You can disable cookies through your browser settings, but some features may not work properly.
